Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

What this application is

Hermes Calendar is a private, single-user tool operated by and for its owner only. It is not distributed, published to third parties, or offered as a service.

Data it accesses

With the owner's explicit consent through Google OAuth, it accesses the owner's own Google Calendar to read, create and edit calendar events. Optionally it may access the owner's own Google Drive, Gmail, Contacts, Sheets and Docs, strictly for tasks the owner initiates.

How data is stored and shared

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer. No data is transmitted to, stored on, or processed by any server controlled by anyone other than the owner. No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party. No analytics or tracking is used.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately invalidates the stored token.
